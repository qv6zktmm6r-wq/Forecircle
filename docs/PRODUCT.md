# Product

ForeCircle connects a golfer asking “Who wants to golf?”, an organizer creating a golf day, and a community saying “This is our Circle.”

Primary language: Golfer, Open Round, Circle, My Circle, Organizer Mode. There is one golfer account. Organizer Mode extends it; no second account, second identity, or separate mobile backend.

Primary domains: ForeCircleGolf.com and ForeCircle.app. Taglines: “Meet. Play. Compete.” and “Find your people. Play more golf.”

The current delivery is the foundation and a browser-local demonstration of subsequent workflows. Every demo page and consequential action identifies that boundary. A local chat message is not real messaging; a local registration is not a booking.

MVP launch requires the 20 user-defined reliable workflows: signup/profile, golfer discovery, posting/joining/chatting around rounds, Circle membership/creation, organizer activation, tournament creation/publication/registration/participant management/waitlists/announcements/results, following/rating organizers, Lab submissions, public sharing and QR codes. These are acceptance requirements, not claims of completion.

Exclude GPS, swing analysis, crypto, unrestricted wagering, fantasy golf, equipment marketplaces, generic video feeds, POS, and tee-time booking. Payments, native apps, leagues, verified identity, and course integrations remain gated.
